## Develop your own campaign plans

- [Free PR Plan Template (HubSpot)](https://www.hubspot.com/resources/templates/pr-plan) - A structured template with guided sections covering situation analysis, SMART objectives, target audiences, PESO tactics, and evaluation metrics.
- [Public Relations PR Plan Template (Asana)](https://asana.com/templates/public-relations-pr-plan) - An operational campaign roadmap template for defining key messages, scheduling embargoes, delegating deliverables, and tracking milestones.
- [PR Strategy Template: Key Steps for Building a Winning PR Plan (Prowly)](https://prowly.com/magazine/pr-strategy-template/) - A step-by-step walkthrough detailing market research, audience profiling, positioning, and tactical channel selection.

## Draft your own crisis communication playbooks

- [Crisis Communications Plans Guide (Ready.gov / FEMA)](https://www.ready.gov/crisis-communications-plan) - An official federal framework detailing stakeholder mapping, notification protocols, information center workflows, and pre-scripted holding messages.
- [Free Crisis Communication Plan Templates (Smartsheet)](https://www.smartsheet.com/content/crisis-communication-templates) - A downloadable toolkit containing vulnerability assessment matrices, incident escalation trees, and spokesperson briefing sheets.
- [CERC: Crisis Communication Plans (CDC)](https://emergency.cdc.gov/cerc/ppt/CERC_Crisis_Communication_Plans.pdf) - A crisis emergency risk communication planning manual outlining response phases, first-hour action checklists, and clearance processes.

## Write your own press releases

- [How to Write a Press Release: The Complete Guide (Prowly)](https://prowly.com/magazine/how-to-write-a-press-release/) - A detailed guide explaining inverted pyramid structure, newsworthiness criteria, quote placement, and boilerplate construction.
- [Event Press Release Template (HubSpot)](https://www.hubspot.com/resources/templates/event-press-release) - A standard template illustrating news release layout, date formatting, executive quotes, and ending symbols.
- [How to Make a Press Release: Examples and Tips (Canva)](https://www.canva.com/learn/how-to-make-a-press-release/) - A structural and visual guide for formatting modern, digital-first press releases with embedded multimedia assets.

## Assemble your own media kits

- [Free Media Kit Template (HubSpot)](https://www.hubspot.com/resources/templates/media-kit) - A customizable template for compiling company histories, executive biographies, key product statistics, and brand collateral.
- [How to Create a Professional Media Kit Online (Canva)](https://www.canva.com/create/media-kits/) - A visual walkthrough and design template library for building multi-page electronic press kits tailored for journalists and creators.

## Conduct your own communication audits

- [Brand Audit: How-To Guide and 10-Step Checklist (Frontify)](https://www.frontify.com/en/guide/brand-audit/) - A 10-step methodology for auditing internal brand adoption, external perception, message consistency, and touchpoint effectiveness.
- [Brand Audit Templates: Reports, Checklists and Presentations (Smartsheet)](https://www.smartsheet.com/content/brand-audit-templates) - Fillable spreadsheet and document templates for conducting channel inventories, competitive messaging reviews, and collateral consistency checks.

## Produce your own measurement reports

- [Interactive Framework (AMEC)](https://amecorg.com/amecframework/interactive-framework/) - The official interactive tool from the International Association for Measurement and Evaluation of Communication for mapping PR activities to organizational outcomes.
- [AMEC Integrated Evaluation Framework Downloads and Templates (AMEC)](https://amecorg.com/amecframework/home/supporting-material/downloads/) - Official blank PowerPoint templates, taxonomy matrices, and scoring sheets for building an executive-ready PR evaluation report.
- [How to Create Media Monitoring Analysis Reports (Meltwater)](https://www.meltwater.com/en/blog/media-monitoring-analysis-reports) - A practitioner guide for structuring media coverage reports, calculating share of voice, conducting sentiment analysis, and presenting findings to executive leadership.

## Craft your own media pitches

- [How to Write a Media Pitch: 7 Real Examples from Experts (Prowly)](https://prowly.com/magazine/how-to-write-a-media-pitch/) - A breakdown of pitch anatomy, email subject line formulas, angle differentiation, and follow-up cadences with real practitioner examples.
- [How to Write a Media Pitch That Gets Results (Meltwater)](https://www.meltwater.com/en/blog/how-to-write-a-media-pitch) - A tactical guide on researching journalist beats, crafting concise 150-word hooks, and structuring interview offerings.
